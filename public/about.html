About SkillSwap

A modern skill-exchange platform for people who learn by teaching.

SkillSwap matches people who want to learn a skill with people who can teach it — then lets them request a swap, chat, and settle the exchange with Skill Credits.

We started from a simple idea: the best teachers are often peers who recently mastered the same thing. Credits keep the system fair. Matching is based on real overlapping skills, not ads.
